This section presents my personal reflections and suggestions — **Vu Thi Phuc**, a student of class **K68CNTTB** at the **Vietnam National University of Agriculture** — after participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program and working on the **Vietnam IT Job Market** project. As a student gradually exploring the field of technology, I aim to gain practical knowledge, improve my technical skills, and build a solid foundation for my future career in data engineering and cloud computing.

### Evaluation by Category

**1. Learning Environment**

I find the learning approach, which combines documentation with hands-on exercises on Cloud Journey, suitable for my learning needs. Exploring individual AWS services and relating them to the project helps me better understand how theoretical knowledge can be applied in practice. I have also realized the importance of proactively reviewing lab instructions, preparing the necessary requirements, and checking configurations to ensure a smoother learning experience.

**2. Learning Materials and Support**

The learning materials are organized into specific topics, making it easier for me to follow the lessons and review information when necessary. During the learning process, whenever I encounter difficulties, I have gradually developed the habit of investigating possible causes, reviewing the steps I have taken, and recording error messages before seeking assistance. I have also become more aware of the importance of protecting personal and sensitive information when discussing technical issues.

**3. Relevance to Information Technology**

The learning content is closely related to several areas of Information Technology, including programming, databases, computer networks, and data storage technologies. Through the **Vietnam IT Job Market** project, I have explored the process of collecting recruitment data from ITviec using Python on EC2, processing JSON data, converting it into Parquet format, and storing it in Amazon S3. The data is then managed through AWS Glue Data Catalog, queried using Amazon Athena, and displayed on a Streamlit dashboard. This experience has given me a clearer understanding of how different components are developed and connected within a data processing system.

**4. Most Improved Skills**

- Improved my ability to independently study documentation and understand the functions of different AWS services.
- Learned how to check IAM permissions, service configurations, storage paths, and logs when troubleshooting issues.
- Gained a better understanding of data storage, processing, and management in cloud computing environments.
- Developed the habit of recording commands, practical results, and errors for future reference and verification.
- Improved my ability to summarize and present knowledge through learning journals, reports, and workshops.

**5. Community Culture**

I appreciate the spirit of mutual support, knowledge sharing, and collaborative learning within the **AWS Study Group** community. For students who are new to AWS, referring to documentation and exchanging experiences with more knowledgeable members can make the learning process more effective. I hope to continue learning from the community, participating in its activities, and sharing the practical knowledge I have gained.

**6. Practical Learning Conditions**

Through AWS hands-on exercises, I have learned that cloud resources must be managed carefully because configuration changes can affect system operations, access permissions, and costs. Therefore, before starting a lab, it is important to define clear objectives, check available resources, and understand how to evaluate the results. After completing the exercises, reviewing and cleaning up unnecessary resources is also an essential practice.

### Feedback Questions

**What Did You Like Most?**

What I appreciate most is the opportunity to connect the knowledge I have learned with a practical project such as **Vietnam IT Job Market**. Exploring the process of storing Parquet data in S3, managing metadata with Glue, querying data through Athena, and visualizing results using Streamlit has helped me better understand the role of each technology. In addition, I have developed the ability to verify results and evaluate the functions implemented during system development.

**What Could Be Improved in the Program?**

- Provide preparation guidelines before each learning module, including prerequisite knowledge, access permissions, and required resources.
- Include more examples of common errors related to IAM, Security Groups, Regions, and S3 paths, along with instructions for identifying their causes.
- Introduce short self-assessment exercises to help learners evaluate their understanding before moving on to the next topic.
- Encourage students to maintain regular worklogs, recording their results and experiences throughout the hands-on learning process.

**Would You Recommend This Program to Your Friends?**

Yes. I would recommend this program to students who are interested in learning about AWS and cloud computing. In my opinion, the program provides opportunities to gain knowledge through hands-on practice while developing independent research and problem-solving skills. However, to achieve good results, participants should actively study the documentation, complete lab exercises, and take notes on important concepts.

### Additional Suggestions

- **Cost and Resource Management Guidance:** Provide additional information about services that may incur costs, how to monitor resource usage, and how to clean up resources after completing lab exercises.
- **More Group Discussion Activities:** Encourage students to present different parts of their projects, such as data collection, processing, querying, and dashboard development, so they can exchange knowledge and receive constructive feedback.
- **Additional Troubleshooting Exercises:** Introduce practical scenarios involving log analysis, pipeline error identification, data verification, and recovery methods in testing environments.
- **Standardized Practice Result Documentation:** Provide guidance on recording execution times, commands, source code versions, and query results to make verification and evaluation easier.

**Would You Like to Stay Involved in the Program?**

Yes. I would like to continue expanding my AWS knowledge, participating in knowledge-sharing activities, and improving my skills in **Python, Data Engineering, and Cloud Computing**. Regarding the **Vietnam IT Job Market** project, I hope to further explore testing methods, improve query performance, and enhance my understanding of operating data systems in real-world environments.
